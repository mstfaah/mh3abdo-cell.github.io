Effective date: 14 November 2026 · App version 2.0.2

Overview

Sky Siege (package com.mrmstfa.skysiege) is an offline air-combat game for a general audience, developed by Mostafa Hussein (Mr Mstfa). The game is not directed at children. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we (the developer) collect

None. The game has no accounts, sign-in, chat, contacts, location, camera or microphone access, and we run no servers or analytics. Your progress (coins, stars, upgrades, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use “Reset progress” in Settings. We never receive, sell or share personal data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free game shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party service: banner ads on menu screens, a full-screen ad between missions (never during combat), an occasional ad when you return to the app, native ad cards clearly labelled “إعلان / Ad”, and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example to revive, double coins or get a free upgrade).

To deliver, measure and protect these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process on your device and Google's servers:

  • the Android Advertising ID (or an app-set ID) and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version) and ad interactions (impressions, taps);
  • diagnostic data such as crash and performance logs of the ad SDK.

This data is collected and used by Google, not by us, under Google's Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ads are limited to the Teen maximum content rating.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where required by law, the app shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform, an IAB TCF-certified consent tool) before ads are requested, so you can accept or refuse personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time from the game: Settings → Privacy & ads (الخصوصية والإعلانات). If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Permissions

The app requests only INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (to load ads) and AD_ID (used by the AdMob SDK as described above). It does not request location, storage, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

Children

Sky Siege is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security and retention

Because we do not collect personal data, we retain none. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes; the effective date above will change accordingly.
