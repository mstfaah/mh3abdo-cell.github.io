Version 1.0.0 · Designed By Mr.mstfa

9 worlds, 270 pictures

From Happy Beginnings and Ocean & Sky to Creative Patterns and Master Art. Every level you finish paints part of the world map.

3 ways to play

Guided follows color hints, Creative lets you choose any colors, Challenge has every tiny detail. Pick Easy, Normal or Master.

Real art tools

Fill, brush, pencil, eraser and color picker, pastel and bright palettes, skin tones, a color wheel, zoom, undo and redo.

Pixi, paint drops and the Lucky Wheel

Pixi cheers you on and collects outfits and colors. Earn paint drops by coloring, spin the Lucky Wheel free every day, and take on a new Daily Challenge.

Made for families

No accounts and no chat. Progress stays on the device. Ads are rated G and never appear while coloring; for under-13s they are child-directed and fewer. Reward videos are always optional, and sharing needs a grown-up.

English & Arabic

Full Arabic with right-to-left layout. Optional one-time Premium removes banner and pop-up ads and unlocks every world.
